Al-Quran ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application Al-Quran (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of data to provide and support the App's features:

  • Location Data — We access your device's approximate or precise location solely to calculate accurate prayer times for your area. Location data is processed on-device and is not transmitted to any external servers.
  • User Preferences — Settings such as language, theme, and prayer calculation method are stored locally on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your device.
  • Bookmarks and Reading Progress — Any Quran bookmarks or reading progress you save are stored locally on your device only.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above exclusively to:

  • Display accurate prayer times based on your location.
  • Remember your preferences and Quran reading progress.
  • Provide and improve the App's functionality and user experience.

3. Data Storage and Security

All data used by the App is stored locally on your device. We do not use remote servers or cloud databases to store your personal data. Location information used for prayer-time calculations is processed on-device and is not transmitted to external servers.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not share your data with third parties. We do not use analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies. No personal data is collected or sent to external servers.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since the information described in this policy is processed or stored locally on the user's device, no personal data is transmitted or stored remotely by the App.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any updates.
