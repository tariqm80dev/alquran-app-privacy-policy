Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Al-Quran ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application Al-Quran (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We use the following types of information to provide and support the App's features:

  • Location Data — We access your device's approximate or precise location solely to calculate accurate prayer times for your area. Location data is processed on-device and is not transmitted to any external servers.
  • User Preferences — Settings such as language, theme, and prayer calculation method are stored locally on your device using local storage. This data does not leave your device.
  • Bookmarks and Reading Progress — Quran bookmarks and reading progress that you save are stored locally on your device only.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above exclusively to:

  • Display accurate prayer times based on your location.
  • Remember your language, theme, prayer calculation method, and other preferences.
  • Save your Quran bookmarks and reading progress.
  • Provide and improve the App's functionality and user experience.

3. Data Storage and Security

The App stores user preferences, bookmarks, and reading progress locally on your device. Location information used for prayer-time calculations is processed on the device and is not transmitted to external servers.

We do not use remote servers or cloud databases to store your personal data. We employ reasonable measures to protect information stored locally by the App.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Location information used for prayer-time calculations is processed on the device and is not stored or transmitted by the App.

User preferences, bookmarks, and reading progress are stored locally on the device and remain there until they are deleted by the user or the App's data is cleared.

Users can delete locally stored App data by using the App's available clear-data or reset functionality, or by clearing the App's data through their device settings.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not share your data with third parties. We do not use analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.

No personal data is collected by the App or sent to external servers.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Since the information described in this policy is processed or stored locally on the user's device, no personal data is transmitted or stored remotely by the App.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

We encourage you to review this page periodically for any updates.
