Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Al-Quran ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application Al-Quran (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of data to provide and improve the App's features:

  • Location Data — We access your device's approximate or precise location solely to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction for your area. Location data is processed on-device and is not transmitted to any external servers.
  • Sensor Data — We access your device's magnetometer (compass) to display Qibla direction. This data is used in real time on-device and is not stored or transmitted.
  • User Preferences — Settings such as language, theme, and prayer calculation method are stored locally on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your device.
  • Bookmarks — Any Quran bookmarks or reading progress you save are stored locally on your device only.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above exclusively to:

  • Display accurate prayer times based on your location.
  • Show the correct Qibla direction from your location.
  • Remember your preferences and reading progress.
  • Improve the App's functionality and user experience.

3. Data Storage and Security

All data collected by the App is stored locally on your device. We do not use remote servers, cloud databases, or any third-party services to store your personal data. We employ reasonable security measures to protect the data on your device.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not share your data with any third parties. We do not use analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies. No personal data is collected or sent to external servers.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since all data remains on the user's device, no child data is transmitted or stored remotely.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any updates.
